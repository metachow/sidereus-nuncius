## Glossary decisions
- "scrupulum primum/minutum" rendered "minute" (or "min."/"sec." where the Latin abbreviates); "hora ab occasu 0, min. 30" = "0 hours 30 minutes after sunset".
- "nebulosa" = "nebula" in \tremph for the two cluster headings; "congeries" = "cluster".
- "vaga sidera / stellae vagantes" = "wandering stars".
- "iuxta longitudinem zodiaci" = "along the length of the zodiac".
