## Glossary decisions
- ether (aethera) kept as "the ether" in p028.01; "horror of night" kept literally.
- Moon, Venus, Earth: feminine pronouns for the Moon and Earth (her), following the Latin personification.
- *specillum* (p028.01) = "lens"; *perspicillum* = "spyglass".
- *asterismus* = "asterism"; *constellatio* = "constellation" (chart title of the Pleiades).
- *magnitudo ultima* = "last magnitude"; *invisibilium prima* = "first of the invisible".
- *Canis* (p026.02) = "the Dog", with footnote identifying Sirius.
- *sextilis*, *quadratura*, *elongatio* kept as "sextile", "quadrature", "elongation".
