# Japanese edition: rulings for proofreading

Decisions taken by the editor after the five translators reported. They override the
translators' choices; apply them across your range.

1. **Cross-references to pages** of the book: the bracketed folio, as the title page
   explains — 「[14v]で述べたように」, never 「14葉裏」.
2. **Observation log** (parts 4 and 5 must read as one text):
   - date and hour at the head of an entry: 「12日、夜の第1時」; later in the same entry
     「第3時」 alone is fine. *hora ab occasu* = 「日没後」; *hora 0, min. 30* = 「第0時30分」.
     A new month once: 「2月1日」「3月1日」.
   - distances: 「東の星は木星から3分離れていた」; with seconds 「1分30秒」; *min. 0, sec. 40*
     = 「40秒」. Chains: 「木星は次の星から3分、この星はさらに西の星から7分離れていた」.
   - *in eadem recta linea* = 「同じ直線上にあった」; *ad unguem* = 「寸分違わず」.
   - *zodiacus* = 獣帯 and *ecliptica* = 黄道, as in the Latin; *secundum (longitudinem)
     zodiaci / eclipticae ductum* = 「獣帯に沿って」「黄道に沿って」; *parallela eclipticae* =
     「黄道に平行な」.
   - weather, figures: 「空は曇っていた」; 「図に示すとおりである」 or the translator's equivalent,
     one wording throughout.
3. *Patavini Gymnasii Publico Mathematico* (f01r.1 and wherever Galileo's post is named) =
   「パドヴァ大学の数学教授」 (he held the public chair; 公式数学者 is wrong).
4. p032.06 *secus a computo astronomico directus*: restore a footnote based on the Chinese
   edition's note there (what the tables predicted for Jupiter in January 1610 and why
   Galileo remarks on it), checked for accuracy.
5. Question marks: where the Latin has a question (including rhetorical ones), end with ？.
6. *quadratura*: of the Moon's phase = 上弦・下弦 (「弦」); as an aspect between bodies =
   「矩（く）」 with the reading at the first occurrence.
7. *philosophi* = 哲学者; *philosophantes* may be 「自然を探究する人々」 where the sense is
   general, 「哲学する人々」 where Galileo means the learned party; be consistent.
8. Footnotes: Japanese style, sentence-final after 。; Latin in `\textit{}`; no claims that
   the notes of the Chinese or English editions do not support, unless you verify them.
9. Medicean: 「メディチ星」「メディチ惑星」; `\tremph{}` only where the Latin has
   `\capsemph{}`.
10. 観測 / 観察: *observatio, observare* (astronomical observation, the log, "observationes
    nostrae") = 観測; looking at or examining something (*intueri, inspicere, spectare*, the
    Moon's surface examined closely) may be 観察 or 見る. One rule for the whole book.
11. Settled from parts 4–5: 世界の中心 (*mundi centrum*), 末日 (*die ultima*), p049.03 keeps
    the Latin 30分 with the note (as the English edition).
12. Magnifications and ratios in running prose in kanji numerals (三倍、千倍近く、六十倍以上),
    as part 1 has them; Arabic numerals only for measured values in the observations
    (dates, hours, minutes, seconds) and for the Italian miles of lunar heights if given
    as a computation.
13. Settled after proofreading: もっとも (not 最も), くぼみ (not 窪み); star counts in kanji
    (二十一個、三十六個); a full-width space after ？ or ！ inside a sentence run (p024.13);
    *sexcentis* = 六百もの (literal); *sextilis* = 離角60度 with the Latin term in the note.
