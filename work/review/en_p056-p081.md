## Changes applied throughout

- **Log time formulas** (editorial ruling): "hora 0, min. 30/40" → "at hour 0 and 30/40 minutes" (p062.01, p065.01, p066.01, p071.01, p075.01, p078.01, p079.01); "hora 1, min. 30" → "at hour 1 and 30 minutes" (p073.01, p074.01); "hora 1, min. 4" → "at hour 1 and 4 minutes" (p076.01); ordinal with minutes "hora prima, min. 30" → "at the first hour and 30 minutes" (p063.01); "hora quinta cum dimidia" → "at the fifth hour and a half" (p064.04, was "At five and a half hours"). Cardinal "hora 1/2/5/6" → "at hour N" and ordinal → "at the Nth hour" were already right. Distances and "0 minutes N seconds" were already right.
- **"in eadem recta"** → always "on the same straight line" (was mixed "same line"/"same straight line": p059.01, p063.02, p064.02, p069.04, p070.02, p070.05, p071.01, p073.02, p074.02, p077.01).
- **orientalior** with three or four eastern stars → "the easternmost" (p061.02, p078.01, p078.03); with two eastern stars "the more eastern one" (p076.01, was "most eastern").
- **The fixed star** used as reference: "the fixed star" throughout (p076.03 "nearer to it" → "nearer to the fixed star"; p075.05 "a certain fixed star" → "a fixed star").

## Translator's doubts resolved

- **p081.02** *modo consequantur, modo praeeant*: "now follow, now precede" is right (in the order of motion along the zodiac: following = east of Jupiter, preceding = west). The period is now split into two sentences, keeping all members.
- **p081.04** *accurate praeadnotatas reversiones perpendenti*: "to anyone who carefully weighs the returns recorded above" (the returns noted in the log, not an earlier text passage). *restitutiones semimenstruas* = returning to its place every half month, i.e. a period of about half a month.
- **p081.08** *arta circularis latio*: *artus* = tight, confined; "a tight circular motion" (a circle as small as these, close about Jupiter, cannot change their distance from the Earth enough). *adeoque … elongari … ut … causam nanciscantur* → "approach and recede … so far as to account for so great a change".
- p076.01 / p070.04 colons: decided by the Latin punctuation (see above).

## Questions for the editor

1. **Ordinal hours without *noctis***: the Latin in this range says only "hora septima", "hora prima" etc.; I kept "at the seventh hour" without adding "of the night" (the January entries in part4 do the same where the Latin has no *noctis*). If the ruling means "of the night" must always be added, it is a mechanical change.
2. **"at the first hour and 30 minutes"** (p063.01) and **"at the fifth hour and a half"** (p064.04): the ruling covers cardinal hours with minutes only; I extended it to these two ordinal forms. Alternative: "at 30 minutes after the first hour".
3. **part4 inconsistency (not edited)**: part4 still has "at 0 hours 30 minutes after sunset", "at 1 hour 15 minutes", "30 sec.", "3 min." etc.; it does not yet follow the rulings, and "after sunset" is an addition to the Latin.
4. p080.01 footnote's second sentence is an interpretation (the tables give only Jupiter's motion); delete it if you want the note purely factual.
